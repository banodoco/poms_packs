# Directing Astrid videos

Read the [visual grammar](../../references/visual-grammar.png) and [composition recipes](../../references/composition-recipes.md). Use the presenter's natural voice; a good technical explanation is not a product trailer.

1. Identify the thing the audience should understand or feel. Choose an opening that shows the thing, not a long logo or capabilities list. For a demo with a reveal, establish the promise and the creator/agent division of labour; demonstrate why the task is difficult; show real intermediate progress; then deliver a result. Do not spoil the strongest payoff in the montage.
2. Make a short shot map with spoken beats, visual evidence, intended composition, motion and sound. Choose a clean static composition first. The [visual language](../../references/visual-language.md) governs the graphic world; the [motion language](../../references/motion-language.md) governs changes. Let the content decide when no effect is appropriate.
3. Use a low-fidelity cut to establish sequence, time and intent before costly detail. Ask for creative feedback on the sequence and selected alternatives. Leave actual implementation and tool choice to the agents; do not misrepresent how the work was produced.
4. Prefer source footage, screenshots, actual generations, comparisons and a real timeline over diagrams that merely imitate product UI. Label explanatory schematics and placeholder material. Mix small payoffs into the explanation. Alternate dense technical sequences with readable, quieter holds; preserve comic timing and cinematic silence.
5. Assemble with the real Astrid timeline, actual pack revisions and managed assets. Use the [catalogue](../../references/effect-catalogue.md), not guessed capabilities. Use the mink only when it interacts meaningfully with the visual story. Finish the video on its strongest idea or payoff and make any invitation brief.
6. Review using the [review skill](../reviewing-astrid-videos/SKILL.md). Confirm media permissions for public delivery. The script, pace and result should be coherent even without knowledge of Astrid.
