# Reviewing Astrid videos

Read the [visual grammar](../../references/visual-grammar.png), [visual language](../../references/visual-language.md) and [motion language](../../references/motion-language.md) and [living pixels](../../references/living-pixels.md). If the mink appears, compare it with the [approved sheet](../../references/mink-character-guide.jpg) and [character notes](../../references/mink-character.md).

Review the actual video at normal speed, then inspect representative settled, mid-transition and boundary frames. Check a phone-sized view and any target portrait format. Report specific timecodes, what the viewer should understand, what is wrong and a small proposed improvement. Separate blockers from taste suggestions. Do not accept a description, source file or attractive single still as proof of a moving result.

- **Narrative:** Is the promise clear, the evidence genuine, the pacing varied and the payoff earned? Does it feel like a human sharing a creative experiment?
- **Static design:** If animation stopped, would the composition still be elegant? Is the hierarchy clear, type legible, imagery uncompromised and whitespace intentional?
- **Motion:** Does the pixel action communicate the change? Are the main surfaces smooth, the effect crisp, the timing intentional, the hold complete and the scene free of accidental blank frames or strobing?
- **Living pixels:** Is the organism doing visible work? Check grasp, contact, effort, coordinated motion, exact authored destination and rest. Does a colony fill the whole footprint behind its cargo, swimming in related currents, with an irregular apron and scouts on multiple sides? Inspect a body-only diagnostic when reviewing a new transport effect. Reject a bottom-edge fringe, decorative halo, generic particles or frame-random flicker. Check that no foreground cell obscures unrelated content.
- **Mink:** Long low body, slender neck, clear dark tail tip, continuous cream underside, clean black outline, short sturdy legs; no invented extra features. Animation should be purposeful, recognisably the same character and not constantly distracting.
- **Production:** Real inputs/outputs rather than invented evidence, correct sources, readable audio, no clipped copy, deterministic seeks, live editor and export checked where available.

Return a short prioritized set of changes; preserve what works. Apply feedback on the next rough cut, and distinguish verified outcomes from things still awaiting host or owner review.

For living media effects, check that an intact slide with reveal disabled still shows convincing support, that the cards can stay entirely still while the organism moves, and that optional cargo drift is slow, bounded and does not obscure text. In a multi-arm display, each current follows the actual card and pools behind its surface. Compare it with the colony: a solid outlined limb, visible palm or fingers, or an unrelated confetti effect fails the shared-material review. See [arm display](../../references/arm-display.md).

## Review a destination-driven sequence

Inspect the resolved storyboard and exact arrival/transition timing before motion; confirm the user's requested content, relative scale and priorities. Inspect transition frames and the actual motion preview for crossings, continuity, support and readability. Check retained media source time, target-attached accents, zero jitter, and any requested intact/reveal mode. Review actual host output separately from standalone samples.
