# Mink character

The owner supplied the [Astrid Character Design Guide](mink-character-guide.jpg) on 24 November 2026. Use it as the canonical appearance reference for this pack; it is included unchanged. It is a reference sheet, not a production sprite asset. Source the approved transparent sprite or sprite sheet from the project/brand assets before animation; do not animate a screenshot of the sheet or treat the old demo mink artwork as approved.

Preserve the approved long, low mink silhouette and slender neck; short sturdy legs, with the front leg slightly forward and hind leg set back; a small rounded ear, blunt nose, slim tapered muzzle, and simple black eye. The continuous cream marking flows from throat to chest to belly without floating between the legs. The orange tail tapers to a distinctly darker tip. Keep the simple consistent one-art-pixel black outline and visible square steps, without anti-aliasing or extra whiskers, fur texture, inner marks or humanlike upright anatomy. The presenting pose is explicitly approved in the reference; use it for purposeful emphasis while preserving the same proportions and identity across views.

Treat the mink as an inhabitant, not a sticker. It may push a card, inspect a result, juggle outputs, trace a connection, pause, look or jump. Give gestures a purpose, anticipation, weight and a resting pose. Keep pixel sprites crisp at native/integer display scale where practical; surrounding media can glide. Use occasional appearances; don't block the content. Match the approved character in all newly authored poses. Unapproved character illustrations in sample media are placeholders only.

For wave and presenting poses, make both forepaws anatomically legible: one deliberate gesture and one visibly attached resting paw. Review the profile silhouette at intended size. Do not erase the second limb just to simplify a pose. The body must stay registered as the gesture changes.
